Klangwerk Privacy Policy

Effective: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what the Klangwerk app ("the app") does with information. Klangwerk is made by MrKeltron ("I", "me"). Contact: keltronprime@gmail.com

THE SHORT VERSION

Klangwerk does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no account. Your settings stay on your phone.

1. INFORMATION THE APP COLLECTS

None. The app does not send any information about you or your device to me or to anyone else.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR PHONE

The app saves its own settings on your phone so they're there next time: your equalizer settings, saved presets, theme choice, and, if you turn on "Remember per device", the names of the audio outputs you've used (for example "Phone speaker" or the name of your Bluetooth headphones). This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup file or share a preset code yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Android's own backup system may include these settings in your personal Google backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is handled by Google, not by me.

3. PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY'RE NEEDED

  • Modify audio settings: needed to apply equalizer and audio effects.
  • Run in the foreground and show notifications: Android requires a visible notification while an app keeps an equalizer running in the background. The notification also gives you quick controls.
  • Start at boot: so the equalizer can turn itself back on after a restart, only if you left it on.
  • Microphone (record audio): only used if you turn on "Live spectrum". Android requires this permission for any app to read the levels of the music your phone is playing. Klangwerk only reads frequency levels to draw the moving bars on screen. It never records, saves or sends any audio, and it never listens to your microphone for anything. The feature is off by default and you can turn it off at any time.

4. TIPS AND PAYMENTS (GOOGLE PLAY VERSION)

The optional tip jar uses Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). I never see your card or payment details. Google may share basic order information with me as the developer (such as an order number, the item, the amount and the country of purchase) for accounting and refunds.

5. CHILDREN

Klangwerk is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. SECURITY

Because the app does not collect or transmit your personal information, there is nothing of yours for me to store on a server. The app does not use the internet, except that the Google Play version talks to Google Play for the optional tip jar.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same place with a new effective date, and the app will include the new text in its next update.

8. CONTACT

Questions about privacy: keltronprime@gmail.com
