Klangwerk Privacy Policy

Effective: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what the Klangwerk app ("the app") does with information. Klangwerk is made by MrKeltron ("I", "me"). Contact: keltronprime@gmail.com

THE SHORT VERSION

I don't collect your personal information, and your equalizer settings never leave your phone. The free Google Play version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below. Buying "Remove ads" turns the ads off, and the ad code then stops requesting ads.

1. INFORMATION I COLLECT

None. The app doesn't send me any information about you, and I don't run any servers, analytics or trackers.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR PHONE

The app saves its own settings on your phone: your equalizer settings, saved presets, theme choice, whether you bought Remove ads, and, if you turn on "Remember per device", the names of the audio outputs you've used (for example "Phone speaker" or the name of your Bluetooth headphones). This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup file or share a preset code yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you have Android backups turned on, Android's own backup system may include these settings in your personal Google backup. That backup is handled by Google, not me.

3. ADS (GOOGLE PLAY VERSION)

The free Google Play version shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • How you interact with ads (for example, when an ad is shown or tapped) and app launch information
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it as described in its policy "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices:

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice any time in Setup > About > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads, or Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off all ads in the app for your Google account.

The sideload version installed outside Google Play has no ads and no ad software.

4. PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY'RE NEEDED

  • Modify audio settings: needed to apply equalizer and audio effects.
  • Run in the foreground and show notifications: Android requires a visible notification while an app keeps an equalizer running in the background. The notification also gives you quick controls.
  • Start at boot: so the equalizer can turn itself back on after a restart, only if you left it on.
  • Microphone (record audio): only used if you turn on "Live spectrum". Android requires this permission for any app to read the levels of the music your phone is playing. Klangwerk only reads frequency levels to draw the moving bars on screen. It never records, saves or sends any audio, and it never listens to your microphone. The feature is off by default.
  • Internet and advertising ID (Google Play version): used by Google's ad and billing software, as described in sections 3 and 5.

5. PURCHASES (GOOGLE PLAY VERSION)

"Remove ads" and the optional tip jar use Google Play's billing system. Google processes payments under its own privacy policy. I never see your card or payment details. Google may share basic order information with me as the developer (such as an order number, the item, the amount and the country of purchase) for accounting and refunds.

6. CHILDREN

Klangwerk is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. SECURITY

I don't store your personal information anywhere. Data that Google's ad and billing software sends to Google is encrypted in transit.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same web address with a new effective date, and the app will include the new text in its next update.

9. CONTACT
